# 💫 About Me:
I’m a passionate Computer Engineering student at MIT Academy of Engineering, Pune, who enjoys turning ideas into meaningful digital experiences. I build modern web applications, AI-powered solutions, and backend systems with a strong focus on both technology and the people who use it.<br>I don’t just write code—I think about the problem behind it. What does the user need? What makes their work easier? How can technology make an experience simpler, smarter, or more useful?<br>With hands-on experience across the stack, I enjoy bridging the gap between design and engineering to create products that are both functional and intuitive. I learn by building, grow through collaboration, and find the greatest satisfaction in seeing an idea evolve from a simple sketch into something real and useful.<br>
